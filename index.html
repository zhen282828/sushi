How to Play

Basics: Match 3+ identical sushi. Idle for 5 seconds for a hint.

Customer System: Each patron brings unique challenges! Check personality traits, difficulty ratings, and reward bonuses before fulfilling advanced orders.

Power-ups (Standard):
🟢 Wasabi Bomb (Match 4): 3x3 blast.
🧴 Soy Splash (Match 5): Clears Row + Col.

Combo Drops (Chain cascades!):
🥢 Rocket (x3): Clears 1 random row & col.
💥 Tempura (x5): Massive 5x5 blast.
🌈 Rainbow (x7): Clears all of one random type.

Combos: Swap ANY two power-ups for huge custom board clears!
