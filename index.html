How to Play

Basics

Tap a tile, then tap an orthogonally adjacent tile to swap. If the swap makes a line of 3+ identical sushi, it clears. Bad swaps just bounce back — no penalty.

Power-ups

🟢 Wasabi Bomb — match exactly 4 in a line. Clears a 3×3 blast when triggered.

🧴 Soy Splash — match 5 or more in a line. Clears its whole row and column when triggered.

Swap a power-up with any neighbor to set it off, even without a normal match.

Orders & Combos

Serve the customer's requested sushi to complete orders for a big score bonus. Chain automatic matches after a swap for rising combo multipliers.

Modes

Arcade: patience drains over time — finish orders to refill it. Zen: no timers, just score and combo chasing.
